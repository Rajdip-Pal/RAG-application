# Benefits Policy

**Document title:** Benefits Policy
**Version:** 1.0
**Applies to:** Full-time employees in India
**Owner:** Human Resources / Total Rewards

## 1. Purpose

This policy describes company-provided health coverage, leave travel allowance (LTA), and related benefits. Amounts below are per employee per financial year unless stated otherwise.

## 2. Health coverage tiers

Employees are enrolled in one health tier based on band. Dependents (spouse and up to two children) are covered under the same tier.

| Health tier | Eligible bands   | Annual sum insured | Dental check-up | Dental implants       | Optical (frames + lenses) | Mental health counselling |
| ----------- | ---------------- | ------------------ | --------------- | --------------------- | ------------------------- | ------------------------- |
| Essential   | Intern, Band A   | ₹3,00,000          | Yes, 1 visit    | Not covered           | ₹5,000                    | 4 sessions                |
| Standard    | Band B, Band C   | ₹5,00,000          | Yes, 2 visits   | Not covered           | ₹8,000                    | 8 sessions                |
| Plus        | Band D and above | ₹10,00,000         | Yes, 2 visits   | Covered up to ₹40,000 | ₹12,000                   | 12 sessions               |

Network hospital cashless access applies to all tiers. Pre-existing conditions are covered after **12 months** of continuous employment.

## 3. Leave travel allowance (LTA)

LTA is paid against actual travel bills for the employee and declared dependents, twice in a block of four calendar years, as per prevailing tax rules.

| Band             | Annual LTA limit |
| ---------------- | ---------------- |
| Intern, Band A   | ₹25,000          |
| Band B           | ₹50,000          |
| Band C           | ₹75,000          |
| Band D and above | ₹1,00,000        |

Unclaimed LTA does **not** carry forward. Advance LTA requires manager approval and tickets in the employee’s name.

## 4. Wellness

The company provides access to a shared wellness app. Gym or home-fitness equipment is **not** a reimbursable benefit under this policy.

## 5. What this policy does not cover

This policy does not define work-from-home stipends, internet reimbursements, or relocation bonuses. Those, if any, are published in separate circulars.
