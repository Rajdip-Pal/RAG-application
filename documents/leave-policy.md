# Leave Policy

**Document title:** Leave Policy
**Version:** 1.0
**Applies to:** Full-time employees in India
**Owner:** Human Resources

## 1. Purpose

This policy describes the types of leave available to full-time employees, how leave is accrued, and how unused leave is treated at year end.

## 2. Leave types

### 2.1 Casual leave (CL)

Employees receive **12 casual leave days** per calendar year. Casual leave is meant for short personal needs and unplanned absences.

Casual leave may be taken in units of half a day or a full day.

### 2.2 Sick leave (SL)

Employees receive **10 sick leave days** per calendar year. Sick leave of more than **2 consecutive days** requires a medical certificate.

Unused sick leave **cannot** be encashed and **cannot** be carried forward.

### 2.3 Privilege leave (PL)

Employees receive **18 privilege leave days** per calendar year. Privilege leave is meant for planned time off.

Privilege leave requests of 3 or more consecutive days must be submitted at least **7 days** in advance.

## 3. Accrual

Leave is credited on **1 January** each year. Employees who join after 1 January receive leave on a pro-rata basis, rounded to the nearest half day.

## 4. Carry-forward and encashment

### 4.1 Casual leave carry-forward

A maximum of **8 days** of unused casual leave may be carried forward to the next calendar year. Any casual leave above 8 days lapses on 31 December.

Carried-forward casual leave must be used by **31 March** of the following year. After that date it lapses.

### 4.2 Privilege leave carry-forward

A maximum of **15 days** of unused privilege leave may be carried forward. Privilege leave above 15 days may be encashed at the employee’s basic daily rate, subject to payroll cut-off dates announced each December.

### 4.3 Sick leave

Sick leave does not carry forward and is not encashable.

## 5. Combining leave types

Casual leave and privilege leave may be combined in a single request. Sick leave may not be combined with privilege leave unless HR approves an exception for a documented medical situation.

## 6. Public holidays

Public holidays are published separately each year and do **not** count against casual, sick, or privilege leave balances.

## 7. Contact

Questions about leave balances should be raised with the employee’s manager first, then with HR at hr@example.com.
