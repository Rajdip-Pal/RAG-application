# IT Security Policy

**Document title:** IT Security Policy
**Version:** 1.0
**Applies to:** All employees, interns, and contractors with company accounts
**Owner:** Information Security

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets minimum rules for using company devices, accounts, and data.

## 2. Accounts and passwords

Work accounts must use SSO where available. Passwords for systems without SSO must be at least **12 characters** and must not be reused from personal accounts.

Employees must not share passwords, OTP codes, or session cookies with anyone, including IT, unless a documented break-glass process is opened.

## 3. Devices

Company laptops must have disk encryption and the standard endpoint agent installed. Personal devices may access email and chat only through approved apps. Local download of customer or employee data onto a personal device is **not** allowed.

Lost or stolen devices must be reported to IT within **4 hours** of becoming aware of the loss.

## 4. Data classification

| Classification | Examples                                       | Allowed storage                              |
| -------------- | ---------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| Public         | Career-site job posts, published blogs         | Any company channel                          |
| Internal       | Org charts, internal wikis                     | Company Drive / approved chat                |
| Confidential   | Salaries, unpublished policies, customer lists | Named Drive folders with restricted ACL only |
| Restricted     | Production credentials, legal holds            | Secret manager / ticketed access only        |

Confidential and Restricted files must not be sent to personal email.

## 5. Software and browser extensions

Only software from the approved catalogue may be installed on company laptops. Browser extensions that read page content require InfoSec review.

## 6. Incident reporting

Suspected phishing, malware, or data leakage must be reported to security@example.com immediately. Employees should not forward the original suspicious email to colleagues.
